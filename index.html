Our Story

A Legacy Built on Wood & Trust

Founded in 1974, Satgure Furniture House has been the cornerstone of quality furniture in Karnal for over five decades. Three generations of master craftsmen have built a reputation that Karnal families trust — generation after generation.

🪵

Premium Wood Selection

We source only the finest sheesham, teak, and mango wood — seasoned and treated for lifelong durability.

✂️

Handcrafted by Master Artisans

Every joint, carving and finish is done by hand by craftsmen who have honed their skills over decades.

🏠

Custom Furniture for Your Home

We build to order — your dimensions, your wood, your finish. Get furniture that fits perfectly.

🤝

Trusted by 10,000+ Families

From Karnal to across Haryana, families have chosen Satgure as their lifelong furniture partner.
